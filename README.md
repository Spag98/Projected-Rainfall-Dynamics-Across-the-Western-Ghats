# Spatio-Temporal Assessment of Rainfall Variability in the Western Ghats (CMIP6)

## Overview
This project analyzes projected rainfall variability across the Western Ghats using CMIP6 climate model outputs (EC-Earth3, SSP245 scenario). The study evaluates spatial differences in rainfall extremes and interannual variability relevant to climate adaptation and flood risk planning.

## Objectives
• Assess long-term rainfall variability (2025–2100)  
• Compare rainfall patterns across North, Central and South Western Ghats  
• Evaluate rainfall extremes using percentile statistics  
• Detect monotonic trends using non-parametric methods  

## Data
Climate projections from CMIP6 EC-Earth3 model (SSP245 scenario).  
Daily precipitation data were aggregated into annual rainfall totals and analyzed section-wise across Western Ghats regions.

## Methods
• Spatial segmentation of Western Ghats into North, Central and South sections  
• Annual rainfall aggregation from climate model outputs  
• Descriptive statistics 
• Mann-Kendall trend test  
• Sen’s slope estimation  

## Key Findings
Central Western Ghats show the highest rainfall extremes (~3013 mm at 95th percentile).  
North Western Ghats exhibit the highest interannual variability.  
No statistically significant monotonic rainfall trend was detected at the 95% confidence level.

## Tools
Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, PyMannKendall, GIS workflows, QGIS, Google Colab.
